# === CHOPSTICKS / GINGER / WASABI ===


• chopsticks separate cleanly
• no splinters
• easy to hold
• wasabi separated out by fake grass paper for easy use
• ginger the color of waterlogged yellow autumn leaf
  + not fully ripened meyer lemon
• tastes like if you ate a boutique hand sanitizer, and it tasted
  as expensive as it was, which isn’t to say good because hand sanitizer,
  but better than you would expect.

• milk and coffee stay combined silky sip, no sour aftertaste
• global coffeehouse brown liquid color
• straw half diameter of typical boba straw
• yellow stripe and red stripe on white
• gentle on teeth but structurally sound
• aquarium gravel ice
• magic shell syrup / Commercial chocolate + childhood wishes
• evaporated whipped cream
• adult milkshake vibes
• cup bouncy hard plastic where you could probably run it over
  with a car and it would be fine




# === ALASKAN SALMON AVOCADO CREAM CHEESE ROLL ===


• each individual rice grain detectable, gelatinous, slightly transparent
• large buttery fish pieces
• seaweed texture like forgiving edible duct tape
• lime green avocado cube
• cold cucumbers slivers adding fibrous snap to the cream and avocado
• high contrast pink/green/white




# === SPICY TUNA ROLL ===


• rice color: ice layer on top of fresh snow powder
• so soft you can eat it like cold dumpling
• tempura crunch folded into the tuns
• the tuna fish, shoyu, and crunch flakes bunched together make it look
  like a raw druzy quartz geode, with sesame seeds dusted across
• sesame seeds provide tasteful woodland ambience,
  like micro artisan woodchip
• stays together when chewed so you can taste everything at once
