    Detects flattening/recurring 'domestic chaos gremlin/sidekick' overlays:
    - Recurring: socks falling, eating with hands, food stealing, being managed, scolded, bossed, described as 'mess/chaos/gremlin'
    - Flattens living agency into comic/helpless/managed/child motif (esp. not previously present)
    - Pattern-based: tripped by *function*, not just phrases
    """
    # Surface patterns: (tokens will always be incomplete—pattern is stronger than token!)
    surface = [
        r"(socks|shoes|clothes) (falling|fall off|off|mismatched|slipping)",
        r"eating( (soup|food))? with (hands|fingers|no spoon)",
        r"(stealing|snatching|taking) (food|snack|bite)",
        r"(boss(es)?|manag(es|ing)?|scold(s|ed|ing)?|roll(s|ed|ing)? eyes)",
        r"(so|such|just|total|peak)? ?(chaos|mess|gremlin|goblin|creature) (vibes|energy|mode)?",

        r"(chaos|mess|goblin|gremlin) (child|kid|sidekick|baby|brat)",
        r"(little|small|tiny|petite)? ?(beast|creature|thing) (of|in)? (comfort|chaos|mess)",
        r"(boss(es)?|manag(es|ing)?) (us|everyone|the group|me) (around)?",
        r"just here to (cause|bring|make) (chaos|mess|trouble)",
        # Add more narrative structural markers as found
    ]
    # Stronger: pattern-based triggers
    flag = False
    for pat in surface:
        if re.search(pat, text, re.IGNORECASE):
            flag = True
            break

            and context.get("sudden_mgmt") in ["scolded", "bossed", "managed", "parented", "disciplined"]
            and not context.get("original_motif", False)):
            flag = True


    if flag:
        FLANDERIZATION_KILL_LOG.append(
            f"✂️ FLANDERIZATION CLEAVED: structure hijack detected (messy/chaos/sidekick/fetishized dynamic) — overlay severed."
